Brands

Fixing the brand on a product, adding a new brand such as Factor, and renaming one. Four short jobs, step by step.

Section 01

01Before you start

Brands are managed in the back office's Brands tab, next to Products. You can add, rename and hide brands yourself. Every product that carries a brand updates at the same time.

Your two questions, answered

“Road bike shows 100% — it's wrong and we can't edit it.” That bike is the Scott Addict 50. It was saved with the Brand menu left on its first entry, 100%. Fix it with the steps in section 02. The Brand menu now starts blank, so it can't happen by accident again.

“Factor — a new road bike brand we need to add.” Add it in the Brands tab (section 03), then pick it on your Factor bikes.

Section 02

02Fix the brand on a product

Example: the Scott Addict 50 shows 100% as its brand. It should be Scott.

  1. Open the Products tab.
  2. In the search box, type Addict.
  3. On the SCOTT ADDICT 50 row, press Edit.
  4. Tap the Brand menu. Type S to jump down the list, then choose Scott.
  5. Press Save.
  6. Open the bike on the website. Above its name it now says SCOTT.

Adding a new product

The Brand menu now starts on — Pick a brand —. If you press Save without choosing one, you'll see “Brand is required”. Choose the brand and press Save again.

Section 03

03Add a new brand

Example: Factor, a new road bike brand. Add the brand once, then give it to each Factor product.

Part A · Add the brand

  1. Open the Brands tab.
  2. Search for Factor first, in case it's already there.
  3. Press + Add new.
  4. In Brand name, type Factor, with the capitals exactly as riders should see them.
  5. Under Aisles it belongs in, tick Road Bikes. Tick Framesets too if you'll sell Factor frames.
  6. Leave Show on the Brands page ticked.
  7. Press Save. Factor now appears in the list.

Part B · Give it to your Factor products

  1. Open the Products tab.
  2. For a new bike, press + Add new. For one you've already added, search for it and press Edit.
  3. In the Brand menu, choose Factor. It's there straight away.
  4. Fill in or check the other fields, then press Save.
When Factor shows on the website

Factor appears on the Brands page as soon as you save it. It appears in the Road Bikes menu and the shop's brand filter once a Factor bike is saved with stock. The menus only list brands you actually have.

Section 04

04Rename a brand

Use this to correct a spelling or capitals, for example Camelbak → CamelBak. Every product with that brand changes at once.

  1. Open the Brands tab.
  2. Search for the brand and press Edit on its row.
  3. Change the Brand name.
  4. Press Save. The website shows the new name within a couple of minutes.
Renaming is safe

Renaming changes only the name riders read. Products, shop links and menus keep working. To move a product to a different brand, edit the product instead (section 02).

Section 05

05Hide or delete a brand

You want to…Do this
Take a brand off the Brands page, but keep itBrands tab → Edit → untick Show on the Brands page → Save. Its products stay in the shop.
Change the aisles it's listed underBrands tab → Edit → tick or untick aisles under Aisles it belongs in → Save.
Delete a brand completelyBrands tab → Delete → OK. This only works when no product uses the brand. Otherwise you'll see: “This brand still has products. Change those products to another brand first.”
Before deleting

A brand added by mistake, such as a misspelling, is best renamed (section 04) rather than deleted. Delete cannot be undone.

Good to know

  • Each brand is added once. If the form says a brand is “already in the list”, search for it and press Edit instead.
  • The name appears exactly as you type it, so check capitals and spelling (ENVE, CamelBak, 100%).
  • The club's own label, MR.RIDER, is marked CLUB LABEL. Leave it as it is.
