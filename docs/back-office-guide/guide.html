Section 01

01Before you start

Everything on the MR.RIDER website is managed from one place: the back office at mr-rider.netlify.app/admin. You never need to write code. You type normal text, pick from lists and press buttons.

Each section of this guide is one job, written as numbered steps. Do the steps in order, one at a time. Words in black boxes are buttons you press, written exactly as they appear on screen.

The four golden rules

1
Nothing is saved until you press Save.

If you close the page or press Cancel, your changes are thrown away.

2
Changes show on the website straight away.

Usually within a minute. Refresh the website page to see them.

3
Delete cannot be undone.

If you are not sure, set the stock to 0 or untick a box instead of deleting.

4
If something looks wrong, stop.

Take a screenshot and send it to your web team with a note of what you pressed.

Phone or computer?

Everything in this guide works on a phone. A computer is easier for adding lots of products or photos at once.

Section 02

02Signing in

The back office is protected by the admin email and password. Only someone with the password can change the website.

To sign in

  1. Open mr-rider.netlify.app/admin in your browser.
  2. You'll see a box titled Admin sign-in.
  3. In Email, type admin@mrrider.lk.
  4. In Password, type the admin password your web team gave you.
  5. Press Sign in.
  6. At the top you'll see Signed in as admin@mrrider.lk · superadmin and a row of tabs. You're in.

Section 06

06Product photos

Every product can have up to 5 photos. The first one is the main photo shown in the shop.

To add photos

  1. Open the product (section 04 for a new one, section 08 for an existing one).
  2. Scroll to Product images.
  3. Press Choose files. On a phone, pick from your photo library. On a computer, hold Ctrl (Windows) or Cmd (Mac) to pick several at once.
  4. Wait until the photos appear and “Uploading…” has gone.
  5. Press Save at the bottom of the form.

To change the order

Use the ← and → buttons under each photo. The photo marked MAIN (the first one) is shown in the shop.

To remove a photo

Press Remove under it, then Save. It is only deleted when you save. Press Cancel instead and it stays.

Rules

  • Up to 5 photos per product. At 5, the Choose files button greys out. Remove one to add another.
  • Each photo must be under 5MB and a JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF file. Normal phone photos are fine.
  • Square or landscape photos on a white background look best.
Where riders see them

The shop shows the main photo, and the second when a rider moves their mouse over it. All 5 photos are on the product's own page, where riders flick through them with arrows or tap the small pictures underneath.

  2. Press + Add a size. A row appears with a Size box and an In stock box.
  3. In Size, type the size as riders should see it: 54, Medium or 42.
  4. In In stock, type how many you have in that size.
  5. Repeat for every size.

The size they pick appears at the top of their message in your Inquiry Inbox, for example Size: 56.

What happens automatically

  • Once a product has sizes, the Stock on hand box disappears. The total is worked out from the sizes and shown under the list.
  • When a size sells out, change its number to 0. Don't remove it. Riders still see it crossed out, so they know you carry it.
  • When every size is at 0, the product shows Out of stock.
  • To stop using sizes on a product, remove every size row. The Stock on hand box comes back. Type the number and Save.

Section 08

08Finding and editing products

The Products tab lists every product, 50 at a time. Search finds any product in a second.

To find a product

  1. Open the Products tab.
  2. Type part of the name or the brand in the search box. The list updates as you type.
  3. Press ✕ to clear the search.

Use Next → and ← Previous at the bottom to see more. Sort changes the order.

To edit a product

  1. Press Edit on its row. The form opens at the top.
  2. Change what you need.
  3. Press Save, or Cancel to change nothing.
Deleting a product

Press Delete on its row, then OK when asked “Delete …? This cannot be undone.” The product and all its photos are removed for good. To take something off sale for a while, set its stock to 0 instead.

Where a product shows on the website

You want it…Do this
On Daily Listings as a new arrivalPress List today on its row. This sets today's date and ticks Daily Listings in one tap.
On the homepageEdit, tick Featured on homepage, Save.
On the Clearance / Sale pageEdit, tick In the Clearance Market / Sale, Save.
Pinned to the top of the shopEdit, type 1 in Running order, Save. Use 2, 3… for the next ones. Leave it blank for every other product.
Daily Listings

Daily Listings shows products listed today or yesterday. Press List today each day on the products you want in that day's drop.

The line under each product name shows its brand, type, stock, sizes (a size marked “(0)” is sold out) and listing date. The tags DAILY, FEATURED and CLEARANCE show where it appears.

Section 10

10Inquiry Inbox

There are no prices on the shop. When a rider presses Club price on a product, a conversation opens here and you reply with the price.

To answer a rider

  1. Open the Inquiry Inbox tab. Needs reply is selected, showing only riders waiting for an answer.
  2. Tap a conversation on the left. It opens on the right and leaves the Needs reply list.
  3. Read the rider's message. If they chose a size, the message starts with it, for example Size: 56.
  4. Type your answer in the Message box.
  5. Optionally press + Attach image / PDF to add a quote, spec sheet or photos.
  6. Press Send reply.

The rider reads your answer on the website, under My inquiries. If email alerts are switched on for the site, they also get an email saying you replied.

Useful extras

  • A thread marked Sinhala — answer in Sinhala means the rider chose Sinhala. Please reply in Sinhala.
  • Tap the product name (with ↗) to open the product in a new tab.
  • Tap the rider's email address to email them directly.
  • When a sale is done, press Close thread. If the rider writes again, it reopens by itself.
  • The Open and All buttons show older conversations.
Attachments

Up to 5 files per message, 10MB each. Photos (JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF) or PDFs.

Section 11

11Membership

People apply to join the club on the website's Apply page. Each application arrives in the Applications tab. Approving one creates a member in Clients.

To approve or reject an application

  1. Open the Applications tab. New applications are at the top, marked new.
  2. Read the card: name, email, phone, what they're interested in, why they want to join, and their address.
  3. Press Approve → create client to accept them. The card then says “Client profile created — see the Clients tab”.
  4. Or press Reject to turn them down.
No “are you sure?”

These buttons act straight away. If you reject someone by mistake, press Reopen on their card to put it back to new.

Clients

  • Lists every approved member. Search by name, email, phone, city or country.
  • To stop a member's access, press Suspend. Press Reactivate to bring them back.
  • Rejecting an application after approving it does not remove the client. Suspend them in Clients instead.

Accounts

Lists everyone who has created a login on the website, including riders who only signed up to ask about products. A new sign-up shows as pending.

ButtonWhat it does
ApproveSets the account to active
RejectBlocks the account
ResetPuts it back to pending

The admin's own account is marked ADMIN and has no buttons.

Section 12

12Enquiries and Coaching

Enquiries

Shows the latest 50 messages sent from the website's Contact page: topic, date, name, email and message. You can't reply from here. Reply from your own email to the address shown. Press Refresh at the top to check for new ones.

Enquiries or Inquiry Inbox?

Enquiries come from the Contact page. The Inquiry Inbox holds conversations about a specific product.

Coaching

The Coaching tab has two lists, each with its own + Add new button.

ListFields
Training programsProgram name, Weeks, Level, Price (USD), Focus (shown on the card)
Coaching poolName, Country, Country code (3 letters, for example LKA), Discipline, Languages, Open slots (0 = waitlist)

Adding, editing and deleting work the same way as products: + Add new or Edit, fill in, Save.

13Common problems

Find your problem in the left column. If it isn't here, take a screenshot and send it to your web team with a short note of what you were trying to do.

ProblemWhat to do
“… is required” appears when I press SaveA field marked * is empty. Fill it in and press Save again.
The website still shows the old versionWait a minute, then refresh the website page. On a phone, pull down to refresh.
The Choose files button is greyThe product already has 5 photos. Remove one first.
A photo won't uploadIt must be under 5MB and a JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF. Screenshot the photo on your phone and upload the screenshot instead.
I pressed Cancel and my new photos have goneCancel throws away everything from that visit to the form. Upload them again and press Save.
Search can't find a productSearch by a word in the name, or the brand. Search does not look inside descriptions.
The Product type I want isn't in the listPick the right Category first. Product types change to match it.
The Stock on hand box has disappearedThe product has sizes, or is set to Pre-owned. Set stock per size.
A product shows Out of stockIts stock, or every one of its sizes, is 0. Edit it and enter the stock.
An aisle has vanished from the website menuThe website hides aisles with nothing in stock. Add stock to a product in that aisle and it comes back.
I deleted something by mistakeDeleting cannot be undone from the back office. Contact your web team straight away.
I'm signed in but see “Members door.”You're signed in with the wrong account. Press Sign out and sign in with admin@mrrider.lk.
